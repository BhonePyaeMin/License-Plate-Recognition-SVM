## images/real/openalpr/ (added 2026-10-29)

444 photos of the OpenALPR end-to-end benchmark (https://github.com/openalpr/benchmarks, folder `endtoend`: 108 European, 222 US, 114 Brazilian photos, one labelled plate each),
downloaded with the permission of the project owner. The repository is licensed AGPL-3.0; the benchmark photos are the OpenALPR project's collection, and they are used here only as
labelled training and test material of this course project (not redistributed outside it). Plate texts and plate boxes (`x y w h`) are the benchmark's own annotations, copied into
`labels.csv` and `plate_boxes.csv` with the file name `openalpr/<country>_<name>.jpg`; every fifth photo of a country is `test`, the others `dev`.
`selectRealPhotos` keeps these photos apart from the 49 Wikimedia photos: all functions use the project photos only unless `'Group','all'` (or `'openalpr'`) is given.
